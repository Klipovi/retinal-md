Referrals

We welcome both self-referrals and professional referrals from healthcare providers

Self-Referrals

If you or your loved one has a retinal condition — such as macular degeneration, diabetic eye disease, retinal detachment, or other conditions — you may contact us directly to schedule a consultation.

Call us at (810) 732-6231 to schedule an appointment. We will be glad to assist you.

You may also ask your ophthalmologist, optometrist, or primary care physician to refer you to our office.

Professional Referrals

Thank you for your confidence in us to assist you in managing your patients. Please do not hesitate to call us at (810) 732-6231 with any question, to refer a patient, or to request information we can provide.

Professional Newsletters

Please contact us to receive quarterly copies of either RETINA Digest (for eyecare professionals) or RETINA Update (for primary care physicians).

If you are a mid-Michigan healthcare professional and do not yet receive a printed copy mailed to your office, call us at (810) 732-6231.

RETINA Digest provides a timely review of recent published articles pertaining to the understanding and treatment of retinal diseases — a fast-reading newsletter to help you keep current on retinal topics pertinent to your patients.
