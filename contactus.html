If you are having a medical issue with your eyes and need to reach our physician, call our main number at (810) 732-6231 and leave a voicemail after the appropriate prompt. Our answering service will forward the message to the doctor, who will call you back.

When Calling, Please Have Ready:

  • Full name, date of birth, and age of the patient
  • The reason for the call
  • Phone number where you can be reached
  • Pharmacy name, location, and phone number (if calling for a refill)
